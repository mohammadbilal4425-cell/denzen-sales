Rules
Booking rules (salesperson):
  1. Sirf woh laptop book ho sakta hai jiska stock mein 1 pcs ho — specs + amount ke saath, 48 hours.
  2. Booking sab ko dikhti hai. Doosra salesperson: pehle 8 hours mein release request kar sakta hai; ya zyada amount par booking kar ke pehli booking fail kar sakta hai; ya 24-hour counter laga sakta hai (holder 24h mein sale na kare to laptop counter wale ko transfer, holder ki fail count).
  3. Fail (time khatam / overtake) hone par salesperson ko poore month mein sirf 1 aur moqa milta hai — 2 fail = us month block.
  4. Sale Entry mein "Stock se minus" karte hi booking khud sold ho jati hai.
